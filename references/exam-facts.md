# UPSC CSE — exam facts to reason with

Verify year-specific numbers (dates, vacancies, cutoffs) against the official notification at upsc.gov.in; everything else below is structural and stable.

## Structure

| Stage | Papers | Marks | Counts toward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prelims** | GS Paper I (100 Qs, 200 marks, 2 h) + CSAT Paper II (80 Qs, 200 marks, 2 h, qualifying at 33% = 66.67) | — | Only qualification to Mains; marks not carried forward |
| **Mains** | Essay; GS I–IV; Optional I & II (250 each = 1750) + two qualifying language papers (English + one Indian language, 300 each, 25% to qualify) | 1750 | Final merit |
| **Interview** | Personality test | 275 | Final merit (total 2025) |

- Prelims negative marking: **one-third of the marks** of a question deducted per wrong answer (GS-I: +2 / −0.667; CSAT: +2.5 / −0.833). Unattempted = 0.
- Both Prelims papers are on the same day (GS-I 9:30–11:30, CSAT 2:30–4:30). Appearing in either paper counts as an attempt.
- Usual calendar: notification Jan–Feb; Prelims late May / early June; Mains Sept; Interview Jan–Apr next year. Dates move: check the official calendar.

## Eligibility (age as on 1 August of the exam year)

| Category | Age | Attempts |
|---|---|---|
| General / EWS | 21–32 | 6 |
| OBC (NCL) | 21–35 | 9 |
| SC / ST | 21–37 | Unlimited within age |
| PwBD (Gen/EWS/OBC) | up to 42 | 9 |
| PwBD (SC/ST) | up to 47 | Unlimited within age |

- "OC" / "Open Category" = General. OBC benefit needs a caste on the **Central** list + non-creamy-layer certificate; EWS needs family income < ₹8 lakh and the asset tests, with a certificate valid for the exam year.
- Born on 2 Aug (year−32) to 1 Aug (year−21) → eligible as General for that year. Compute remaining attempts as min(attempts left, eligible years left).
- Educational qualification: any bachelor's degree (final-year students may apply for Prelims, must prove the degree before Mains).

## GS-I Prelims subject behaviour (typical ranges, vary each year)

| Subject | Typical Qs | Format tendencies |
|---|---|---|
| Polity | 12–18 | Statement-based; procedural details (who/how/majority/time limits); constitutional vs statutory bodies |
| Economy | 12–18 | Concepts (inflation, monetary tools, BoP) + schemes/indices in news; "how many of the above" |
| Geography + mapping | 10–18 | Physical processes, Indian rivers/passes/ranges, climate; map-based matching |
| Environment & Ecology | 10–18 | Protected areas, species + IUCN status, conventions, pollution terms, climate initiatives |
| History (Modern + A&C + Ancient/Medieval) | 12–20 | Modern: movements, acts, personalities; A&C: temple styles, dances, paintings, Buddhism/Jainism |
| Science & Tech | 6–12 | Current-affairs hooked: space, biotech, defence, IT terms; basic NCERT science |
| Current affairs & schemes | 15–25 (overlapping) | Schemes with ministry/beneficiary details; reports and their publishers; international groupings |

Cutoffs: General category GS-I cutoff has typically sat in the high-80s to ~100 out of 200 in recent years; it moves every year with paper difficulty. Never quote a specific figure as fixed; say "typically around" and point to the official cutoff PDF.

## What decides Prelims

1. **Accuracy on attempted questions** (70%+ is the target); attempt 75–90 questions depending on confidence.
2. **Elimination skill** built from mocks: absolute words ("only", "all", "always", "never", "directly enforceable") are usually wrong; extreme or over-specific statements are suspect; if two options are near-identical, one of them is usually right.
3. **Revision count**, not source count. One source, 4 revisions, beats four sources once.
4. **CSAT competence** — scores of 60–70 fail. One PYQ paper early to diagnose; weekly practice if below 80.

## Common failure patterns to steer learners away from

- Collecting PDFs and following several teachers at once instead of finishing one book.
- Reading newspapers for 2–3 hours and taking notes on everything.
- Starting the optional or Mains answer writing before Prelims when the Prelims is < 7 months away.
- Skipping mocks until "the syllabus is finished" (it never is).
- Ignoring PYQs because "UPSC doesn't repeat" — it repeats themes, not questions.
- Not planning leave for the final 2–3 weeks.
