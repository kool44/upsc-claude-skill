im preparing for upsc mains, can you check this gs2 answer like an examiner and tell me what marks i would get. question was "Citizen's Charters have not delivered the accountability they promised. Critically examine. (10 marks, 150 words)". my answer:

Citizen charter is a document which tells the citizens about the services of a government office and the time in which they will be given. It was started in UK by John Major in 1991 and India adopted it in 1997. Citizen charters are good because they make government offices accountable and transparent and the citizen knows what to expect. But there are many problems. Many charters are just made for formality and not followed. The staff is not trained and there is no punishment if the charter is not followed. People also dont know about the charters. So the charters have not delivered accountability. Government should make them legally binding and there should be training and awareness so that charters can succeed. Then citizens will get good service and corruption will reduce and India will develop.
